# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short.

**Be decent.** Assume the other person is acting in good faith, is busy, and
may not share your first language. Criticise code, not people.

**Not welcome:** harassment, slurs, personal attacks, sexual attention,
posting someone's private information, or sustained disruption of a
discussion.

**Reports.** If someone is behaving badly, open a private report by emailing
the maintainer listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or contact GitHub
Support if the maintainer is the problem. Reports are handled privately.

**Consequences.** Depending on severity: a warning, deletion of the offending
comment, or being blocked from the repository. The maintainer decides, and
does not have to litigate the decision in public.

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages and any other space
the project uses.

> If you would prefer a fuller, standard document, the
> [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) is a good
> drop-in replacement for this file.
