Lock In is a focus timer built on the pomodoro method: you work for a set
stretch, then you take a break, and the app keeps track so you do not have to.

It is completely free. There is no account to make, no subscription, no
adverts, no "pro" tier and no feature held back for payment. There is also no
tracking of any kind: the app has no network code at all, so nothing you do
inside it goes anywhere.

FIVE TIMER LENGTHS
Espresso (15 minutes), Classic (25), Deep (50), Flow (90), and a Custom one
where you set the focus, break and long break lengths yourself.

A CHIME YOU WILL NOT MIND HEARING
Every chime is generated by the app rather than played from a recording, so it
is clean and small. Choose a soft bell, a singing bowl or a wooden block.
Focus ends on a lower note than a break, so you can tell them apart without
looking at the screen.

DEEP NOISE, RAIN, OR A LOW DRONE
Optional background sound while you work, with its own volume and a tone
control that runs from muffled and distant to fully open. Every sound is
generated as it plays, so none of it ever loops. It fades in and out gently
and stops on its own when the session does.

BREAKS THAT LOOK AFTER THEMSELVES
Breaks can start automatically when focus ends. Focus does not start
automatically unless you ask it to, so a break ends when you are ready.

LIGHT AND DARK
Follows your system setting, or stays on whichever you prefer.
